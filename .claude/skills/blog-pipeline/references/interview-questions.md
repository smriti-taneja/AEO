# Interview gate — questions and push-back rules

Ask all of these before research or writing starts. Where a "push back if" condition is listed, do not accept a thin answer — ask a sharper follow-up until you get one.

1. **Target keyword.** Push back if it's a topic, not a keyword ("marketing" instead of "b2b marketing attribution software").
2. **Audience.** Must name role, stage, and pain — not "marketers" or "businesses". Push back until you get something like "series A growth marketer, evaluating tools, frustrated by attribution gaps between paid and product-led signups."
3. **Search intent.** Informational / comparison / decision. If they don't know, infer from the keyword and confirm with them rather than guessing silently.
4. **Pillar only, or pillar + clusters.** If clusters: how many (default 3–4).
5. **Cluster angles.** Each cluster needs its own distinct angle, not "more of the same topic." Push back if two angles are effectively the same query.
6. **Competitors to beat.** Named domains/URLs, not "the big players" or "everyone in the space."
7. **Their unique angle.** Push back on generic differentiation ("we're better", "we care more") — ask for a specific mechanism, data point, or story competitors can't claim.
8. **Internal links to existing posts.** Ask for actual URLs/slugs if they have an existing site. If none exist yet, note that and move on.
9. **Visual needs.** Infographics, screenshots, or neither — and if screenshots, of what.
10. **Author.** A named person (for the byline/E-E-A-T signal), not "the brand."
11. **Publish target.** Draft only, or a specific date. Either is fine — but get an explicit answer, not silence.
12. **Anything to avoid.** Competitor name-drops, compliance-sensitive claims, banned phrases beyond what's already in `wiki/voice/banned-phrases.md`, etc.

Record the final answers to `pipelines/blog/<slug>/brief.md` before moving to research.
