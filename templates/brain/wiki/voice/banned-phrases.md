# {{BRAND}} — Banned phrases and words

Add brand-specific bans below the defaults. The `copywriting` skill enforces this list on every draft.

## Always banned (default — applies to every project)
- Em dashes used as a dash (`—`, `--`) — no exceptions
- "In today's fast-paced/ever-evolving world..."
- "In this article, we'll explore/dive into/unpack..."
- "It's important to note that..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "Game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "robust", "leverage" (as a verb), "synergy"
- "Studies show", "experts agree", "research indicates" — without a specific named, linked source
- "In conclusion..." as a closing-paragraph opener
